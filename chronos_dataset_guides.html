Step-by-step guide for your five attached datasets

Each section below is one complete click-through of Chronos Studio for one CSV: what to upload, what to leave alone, which cards to accept, which toggles to set, and what the software shows at every stage from S0 to S15.

Stage navigation and locked Apply. S6 and S7 intentionally share one results panel. The v3 source no longer lets a background refresh move the stepper from S6 to S7, or from S2 to S3 after S3 has completed; scrolling stays on the stage you chose. The selected stage is also saved per session, dataset and series version, so a page reload returns to S2 or S6 instead of the backend's last-completed stage. S0 — Load data

Click the stage bar's S0 Data (or ribbon Upload file). On the Data panel choose the Upload a file tab, click choose a file, and select catfish.csv. The app reads it immediately; you do not need to press a separate load button.

Open Change mapping and confirm these fields:

Time columnDate
Value columnTotal
Calendarleave — infer from the dates — (or explicitly choose Monthly (start of month) (period 12); both give MS).
Duplicate timestampsAverage them (default).
Data classleave — keep the detected class —; the quality card says continuous.

Click Apply mapping only if you changed something. Read First rows, as the app sees them and the Data quality report: 324 observations, 0 gaps, regular monthly index.

S1 — Explore (EDA)

Open S1 Explore. It computes automatically; there is nothing to tick. Leave the rolling window box blank. Optionally click Re-run to refresh. Read the Summary, Descriptives, Plots, Outliers and Data quality cards. The profile finds strong monthly seasonality and no missing values.

S2 — Preprocessing advice

Open S2 Advice. It runs automatically. You will see one high-confidence recommendation:

Keep a seasonal term in whatever model you choose. Because: seasonal strength F_S = 0.81 at period 12 with Kruskal–Wallis p < 0.001; the conventional boundary for strong seasonality is F_S = 0.64.

Follow it as a modelling instruction, not as a data edit. There is no Apply button on this card, because it does not change the series. Remember it in S9/S10: keep seasonal candidates such as SARIMA and seasonal ETS ticked. Do not transform or delete anything here.

S3 — Prepare data, first pass

Open S3 Prepare. The Applied strip should say Nothing has been changed yet and show series version 1. Leave it unchanged for now. This is the panel where treatments will later appear; its ↶ Undo last and ↷ Redo last buttons replay the chain from the untouched original. The Preview, Before you commit, Apply and Discard controls live on the S2 cards, not as free-form inputs here.

S4 — Decompose

Open S4 Decompose. In the Method bar click STL (recommended), leave Model: additive, then click Run. The additive scorecard reads roughly 90% trend, 8.3% seasonal, 2.1% remainder. You may click Compare methods to see classical/UCM/HP overlays; all values are positive, so multiplicative also runs (89% trend, 8.0% seasonal), but keep additive as the recorded result so the later steps all describe the same stored decomposition.

S5 — Test stationarity

Open S5 Stationarity and click Run the tests. There are no checkboxes in this panel. The software reports Seasonal unit root, with 2 contradictions and estimated breaks at 2003-03-01 and 1993-11-01. Read What to do about it and Where the tests disagree; do not delete the break dates, because the software hands them forward as step dummies.

S6 — ACF / PACF

Open S6 ACF/PACF. It loads automatically with max lag 48; no input is needed. The reading is seasonal spike at 12 (r = 0.813, with lag 24 also outside the band). Read Candidate model orders — starting points and What to do next. If the panel says the series changed, click Re-run.

S7 — What next: clear the S8 blocker

Open S7 What next. The headline is Next step: take a seasonal difference at the detected period — not a first difference. On the first card click Preview this; the verdict is better, so click Apply. Do not apply the secondary Both differences are needed note; it has no operation.

After applying, go back to S1 and click Re-run, then re-run S2, S4 Run, S5 Run the tests, S6 Re-run and S7 Re-run. S3 now shows one treatment, difference, series version 2, and 12 missing values at the start of the series.

S3 again — fill the 12 gaps

Re-opened S2 now contains a medium-confidence card Impute with a state-space model. Click its Preview, read Before you commit, then click Apply. Ignore Keep the unusual points…, Do not add seasonal terms, A difference has already been applied and Smoothing for presentation: they are notes or optional display changes, not needed for this run.

Use Re-run Steps 1 and 2 in S3, then re-run S4–S7. The Applied strip now lists difference + kalman_smoother, series version 3, 0 missing.

S8 — Ready to model?

Open S8 Gate. In Horizon type 12, click Check this horizon, then Assess readiness or Re-assess. The verdict changes to READY WITH CAVEATS, with no blockers. Read Caveats — These travel with every later output: the 2 structural breaks and the differenced/imputed series are part of the result, not errors to clear.

S2 — Preprocessing advice: which card to follow, and why

S2 Advice shows three high-confidence cards:

Take logs before modelling. Because: Box–Cox λ = 0.22 (95% interval −0.07 to 0.5), the seasonal swing widens as the level rises, and level–spread correlation is 0.76.
Keep the unusual points and add them to the model as intervention dummies. Because: 7 observations look unusual once trend and season are removed, 1 of them a level shift; deleting them removes information.
Keep a seasonal term in whatever model you choose. Because: seasonal strength F_S = 0.95 at period 12, Kruskal–Wallis p < 0.001, above the 0.64 boundary.

Decision for a full S0→S15 run: do not click Apply on the log card here. It is valid advice for a variance-stabilised model, but this build refuses to forecast a series that has both an active transform and a difference; S7 will need a seasonal difference to clear the S8 blocker. Leave the outlier points in the data (do not delete them) and remember the seasonal-term card when ticking models in S10. The outlier-flag card can be previewed and applied if you want the intervention dummies recorded, but it is not required to reach the forecast.

If you do apply the log, S3 will show transform.log and series version 2; re-run S1–S7 before trusting their numbers. An active log plus a S7 seasonal difference is refused at S13: a back-transformed value would be a ratio of changes, not a level forecast. For a full forecast in original units, start clean from raw version 1 and apply only the seasonal difference and imputation, or undo the whole transform/difference/imputation chain and re-fit before S13.
